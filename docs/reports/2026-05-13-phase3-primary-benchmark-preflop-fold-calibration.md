## Audit finding

A targeted audit of seed 5 and seed 2 showed that the dominant soft-warning pattern was not illegal action or transcript leakage. It was repeated preflop `overfold` flags generated by the decision evaluator:

```text
Folded despite equity being far above the price offered by pot odds.
```

Examples included speculative or weak suited/preflop holdings such as:

- `Q♠ 6♠`
- `9♦ T♣`
- `5♦ 4♠`
- `2♣ 4♦`
- `8♥ 3♥`

With the practical benchmark execution profile (`ai_equity_simulations=5`), raw preflop equity estimates are intentionally low-rollout and can be noisy. Penalizing every preflop fold solely because noisy raw equity appears far above pot odds overstates fold mistakes for non-premium hands.

## Change made

Added a focused decision-evaluator calibration:

- Preflop folds with known hole-card shape are no longer marked as `overfold` from noisy raw equity alone unless the hand is independently strong enough that folding is clearly bad.
- Premium/strong preflop folds still trigger `overfold`.
- Postflop and unknown-hole-card overfold behavior remains unchanged.

New regression tests:

- `test_preflop_suited_weak_fold_is_not_overfold_from_noisy_raw_equity_only`
- `test_preflop_premium_fold_still_flags_overfold`

## Interpretation

The full primary Phase 3 benchmark now has a clean machine-readable PASS under the practical benchmark execution profile. This does not mean AI strategy quality is complete; it means the benchmark gate is now strong enough to serve as a repeatable baseline for the next Phase 3 work.

Recommended next work:

1. Add progress/incremental artifact writing for long benchmark runs.
2. Start profile calibration against explicit target bands rather than only separability spread.
3. Audit the remaining poor/blunder cases for high-impact strategy improvements.
4. Keep online/API/WebSocket/auth/platform implementation deferred until Phase 3 evaluation-loop hardening remains stable over repeated runs.
