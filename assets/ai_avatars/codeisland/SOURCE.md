# CodeIsland mascot assets

The GIFs in this directory are the original mascot animations from
[wxtsky/CodeIsland](https://github.com/wxtsky/CodeIsland/tree/main/docs/images/mascots),
retrieved on 2026-10-16. Their first frames are the source for the terminal
sprites in `texas_holdem/ui/ai_avatar_data.py`. They are used under the MIT
license in `LICENSE`.

`comparison.png` places each original first frame above its 24×18 terminal
sample so the loss of detail is visible before changing the renderer.

The mascot names are visual themes for poker opponents; they do not identify
the decision engine controlling those players.
